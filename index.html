Signal methodology & safeguards

HIKEYFX calculates SMA-seeded EMA, Wilder RSI and ATR, checks trend alignment across 15m/1h/4h, detects recent swing levels and basic engulfing candles, then applies momentum and volatility filters. It returns WAIT when evidence conflicts or data is insufficient. The score is a rule-confluence score, NOT a probability of winning.

No indicator can guarantee 97% accuracy or profitability. Backtest results depend on data quality, spread, slippage and market regime. Validate on unseen data and paper trade before risking money. This prototype does not connect to MT5, place trades or access your broker account.
